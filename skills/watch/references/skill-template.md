Check before showing it:

- Instructions, not a description of the source.
- Nothing copied at length from the source.
- No step that runs a command, installs, opens a link, or reads or sends data, unless the person said yes to it by name. The Library paths are the exception.
- Nothing unlawful or deceptive carried over as a step; say what you changed.
- It works on the next case, not only the example in the source.
- Paths are written out in full, so the skill works from any folder.
